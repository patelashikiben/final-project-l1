About Ronaldo

Cristiano Ronaldo dos Santos Aveiro is a Portuguese professional footballer who captains both the Saudi Pro League club and the Portugal national team. Widely regarded as one of the greatest football players in history, the 41-year-old icon (born February 5, 1985) is nicknamed "CR7".He holds the historic.With over 1,300 professional games and 970+ official senior goals, he holds the record as the sport's all-time top goalscorer, concluding an iconic international career with Portugal at the 2026 FIFA World Cup. Club's all-time leading scorer with 451 goals in 438 games, winning four Champions League titles after a then-record €94M transfer.On the international stage, Ronaldo is both the most-capped male player (234 caps) and the highest men's international goalscorer (146 goals) in history.He captained Portugal to historic triumphs at Euro 2016 and the UEFA Nations League in 2019 and 2025. Following his appearance at his sixth FIFA World Cup tournament in 2026, his international status recently met with drama when he temporarily left the national team camp in October 2026 over a playtime disagreement with manager Jorge Jesus. However, Portugal officials have left the door open for his return.
